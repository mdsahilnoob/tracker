# FocusFlow Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 9, 2026

FocusFlow is an offline-first focus timer and productivity app. This Privacy Policy explains how FocusFlow handles information in the current app version.

## Information we handle

FocusFlow does not require an account and does not send your productivity data to the developer or to a FocusFlow server. The app does not currently use advertising, analytics, tracking, a backend, remote push notifications, or in-app user profiles.

The app may store the following information locally on your device:

- focus session start time, end time, planned duration, actual duration, and completion status;
- task titles, task completion status, and task timestamps;
- timer state, including the selected task and pause information; and
- preferences such as focus duration, daily goal, theme, accent color, and haptic feedback preference.

Task titles and session information may contain personal information if you choose to enter it. FocusFlow uses this information only to provide the timer, task, insights, history, streak, and goal features.

## How local storage works

The information listed above is stored in the app’s private local storage using the device’s application storage. It is not transmitted by FocusFlow to the developer or shared with other users.

FocusFlow does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or general device files. The current build uses haptic feedback when you enable it. Notifications are temporarily disabled in the current Expo Go-compatible build.

## Sharing and disclosure

The developer does not sell, rent, or share your FocusFlow productivity data with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

The operating system, Google Play, Expo Go, or other platform services may process technical information needed to install, update, secure, or operate the app and their services. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. FocusFlow does not control or receive the data handled independently by those platforms.

## Retention and deletion

You control the locally stored FocusFlow data:

1. Open **Settings** in FocusFlow.
2. Select **Delete all focus data**.
3. Confirm the deletion.

This removes the locally stored tasks, focus sessions, active timer state, and resets the app’s settings. You can also clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app through your device settings. Device backups may retain app data according to your operating-system backup settings, so delete the data in FocusFlow before uninstalling if you want to remove it from the app first.

Because FocusFlow does not maintain an account or server copy of your productivity data, there is no separate server account to delete. If you need help with deletion, contact us at [Your privacy contact email].

## Security

FocusFlow keeps productivity data in the app’s local storage and does not transmit it to a FocusFlow server. No method of storage is completely risk-free. You are responsible for protecting access to your device, including its passcode, biometric lock, and backup settings.
